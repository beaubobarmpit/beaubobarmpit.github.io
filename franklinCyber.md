🐒💻 Franklin Cyber Loco 💻🐒

THE ACTUAL GAMEPLAY
Game Summary: Save SpongeBob from the Erupting Volcano!
You are Franklin the monkey, and this time you’ve been sucked into a computer motherboard! The maze is full of locked doors, cybersecurity questions, and bananas. Your mission is to collect enough bananas, defeat the cyber boss, and escape back to your home world.
Objective:
Explore the motherboard maze
Collect bananas around the maze
Unlock doors by answering educational cybersecurity questions
Collect enough bananas to access the boss room
Defeat the cyber boss by answering harder questions to escape
Core Gameplay Loop:
Navigate the maze as Franklin
Collect bananas to progress
Find locked doors
Answer cybersecurity questions to unlock new paths
Reach the boss room
Defeat the boss and escape the maze
Challenges:
Navigating confusing maze paths
Answering questions correctly
Managing progression to reach the boss room
Incorrect answers may slow progress or block paths, requiring exploration of other areas.
Tips for Success:
Explore Carefully: Some paths lead to bananas or doors, others lead to dead ends.
Think Before Answering: Correct answers help you progress faster.
Collect Enough Bananas: You must meet the banana requirement before entering the boss room.
Educational Element:
Franklin Cyber Loco teaches basic cybersecurity concepts through interactive questions. Players learn while progressing through the maze and defeating the boss.
CONTROLS:
Movement:
WASD – Move Franklin around the maze
Space – Jump
Camera:
Mouse Movement – Look around
Scroll Wheel – Zoom in and out
Interaction:
E – Interact with doors / answer questions
